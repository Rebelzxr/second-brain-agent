# Digest

One source in, one digest note out, filed and linked so an agent can find it later.

## Steps

1. **Get the whole source.** Read the full file or page. If you only got part of it, say so at the top of the note.
2. **Check the vault first.** Search `2_Wiki/INDEX.md` and `2_Wiki/Digests/` for the same source or topic. Update an existing digest rather than duplicating it.
3. **Write the digest** from `Templates/digest.md` into `2_Wiki/Digests/YYYY-MM-DD - <Source> - <Title>.md` (the date is the source's own date).
4. **Fact-check the top three claims** against the original or a primary source. Record what was confirmed, what was not, and what could not be checked.
5. **Link it.** Add `[[links]]` to related concepts and digests. Add a line to `2_Wiki/INDEX.md` under Digests.
6. **Look for a pattern.** If the same idea now appears in three or more digests from the last 30 days, propose a `2_Wiki/Compounds/` note to the user with the links. Do not write it without a yes.
7. **Reply** with the note path, the TL;DR and anything the user should decide.

## Sections (in this order)

- **TL;DR:** three bullets.
- **What it says:** a fair summary in your own words.
- **Distilled knowledge:** the reusable part: frameworks, numbers with their source, steps.
- **Fact-check pass.**
- **How this affects me:** a table of area, change, impact and action. Every row has a concrete action, or it goes.
- **Steal / Ignore:** what to adopt, and what does not apply and why.
- **Questions:** what the source leaves open.
- **Related:** links.

## Voice and honesty

- Plain words, short paragraphs, outcome first.
- Paraphrase. Quote at most one short line, with credit.
- Keep "the source says", "I infer" and "unknown" separate. Never turn a creator's claim into a fact without checking it.
- Nothing private in the note: no client names, money figures or personal messages.
